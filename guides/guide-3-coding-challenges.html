Hint 1: what repeats?

Hint 2: find a value inside an item

In a different list, parcels[i] means the current parcel. A dot picks a property: parcel.label reads its label. Which list and which numeric property does your activity need? Join the ideas yourself.

10% means one part out of ten equal parts. Start with a £10 subtotal. Work out the value of one part.

Different worked example: 20% of £10 = 10 × 0.20 = £2. The amount left is £8.

  1. Choose the fraction for your 10% task. A calculator is fine.
  2. Replace only the 0 on the E2 line. Return the discount, not the amount left to pay.
