Help: background images and CSS syntax

Background images are relative to style.css. Use your own file in project/images/. In the app the path begins images/; the lab uses a different path. Do not copy the lab path into your app.

/* Separate syntax example, not a completed challenge */
.sample {
  border-width: 2px;
}

.sample selects a class. Inside the braces are property/value pairs. Keep semicolons between them. In your file, preserve the supplied selectors and edit only values.

No change? Check that you saved style.css, refreshed the project tab and added personal-design. High contrast deliberately overrides decorative colours/images.

Optional: a web font

After the system fonts work, choose a font from Google Fonts. Use its “Get font” / embed option. Put its supplied link in the head of index.html, before the style.css link. Put the supplied font-family in your MY DESIGN heading rule, keeping a fallback such as sans-serif. Save both files and refresh. Test with the network disconnected; the fallback should remain readable.

4 · Solve a design problem

Choose a goal: make a crowded heading easier to read; give your app a calmer mood; or keep text readable over a picture.

  1. Capture a “before” screenshot.
  2. Choose and test your own CSS changes. A plain background may work better than a busy photo.
  3. Narrow the browser window. Check words fit and controls remain readable.
  4. Capture “after”. Explain what improved for your audience.

Check: A partner can read the heading and find an item, its price and the tray. Make one useful improvement.
